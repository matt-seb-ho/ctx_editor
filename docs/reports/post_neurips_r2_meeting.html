The smoking gun (database)

User asks for AVG/MIN/MAX of singer age in France. Rewrite v2's compacted spec says "include AVG/MIN/MAX of age and all relevant columns." Assistant outputs SELECT *, AVG(Age) OVER (), MIN(...) OVER (), MAX(...) OVER () — a window-function dump of every column. v1 produced a clean aggregate. The "Be EXHAUSTIVE" instruction in v2 backfires.

Reset wins because it pipes the analyzer's output into a templated context message without a second LLM "interpret-what-the-user-wants" pass. Rewrite's flexibility is the source of its lossiness.

Two observations:

4. Narrative for the paper

  • Reset wins last-turn replay (LiC). Augment-style appended notes aren't enough when the test only fires once and the test set is dominated by samples where prior assistant turns are actively wrong.
  • Augment wins multi-turn (WildChat, CollabLLM-math-hard). Keeping both the (mostly fine) assistant turns AND the analyzer's notes is the right setting when the conversation is already mostly clean.
  • Reset wins multi-turn-hard (CollabLLM-bigcodebench). Hard tasks need the harder intervention — drop the polluted prior turns.
  • Rewrite is consistently worst. The second LLM compaction call introduces F4 overfit, regardless of prompt design.

So the paper-ready ranking is: (Augment ≈ Reset) ≫ Baseline ≫ Rewrite, with Augment winning on easy/clean and Reset winning on hard/dirty.

6. Discussion items for the meeting

  1. Is the Augment-vs-Reset story strong enough? It's clean on WildChat (3 models) and CollabLLM math-hard, but inverts on CollabLLM bigcodebench. The "appropriate-intensity" framing handles this, but it's a more nuanced story than "AC3 wins".
  2. Do we want to spend more time on Rewrite? The v2 negative result is informative but not flattering. Options:
    • Drop Rewrite from the paper entirely (it underperforms; not our recommended method).
    • Keep it as the "naïve baseline" the paper deliberately avoids.
    • Try a v3 (quoted-user-message anchoring) — but the per-task pattern suggests it'd just shift the failure mode.
  3. Is the Kimi-Huang shrinkage a problem? AC3-Reset wins 71.6% on Kimi vs 89.8% on gpt-5-mini. Could be a ceiling effect (Kimi's AO is already strong) or test-set artefact (samples were chosen on gpt-5-mini's failures).
  4. Should we run multi-rep on CollabLLM? The N=1 single-rep is enough for the +50–80pp directional story but not for the 85% vs 95% kind of comparisons.
