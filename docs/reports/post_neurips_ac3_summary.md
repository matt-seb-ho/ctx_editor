## Phase 1 — DeepSeek-V4-Flash, last-turn replay, n=3 prefixes

Full table: `docs/reports/post_neurips_ac3_phase1.md`.

| Strategy | math | code | database | actions | Δ vs Baseline (avg) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Baseline | 72.2 | 34.7 | 22.4 | 76.0 | 0 |
| **AO** (assistant omit) | 86.1 | 60.3 | 45.6 | 86.0 | **+18.1pp** |
| Augment | 84.0 | 58.7 | 41.5 | 84.0 | +15.7pp |
| **Reset** (always-on) | 81.9 | 59.5 | 49.0 | 83.3 | **+17.1pp** |
| Gated-Reset | 82.6 | 55.9 | 49.7 | 85.3 | +17.0pp |
| Rewrite (LLM compaction) | 73.6 | 28.6 | 27.9 | 74.0 | -0.3pp |

**Takeaways**:

- **AO is the practical upper bound** (+18.1pp), confirming the
  paper's framing: the assistant's own prior turns are the main
  bottleneck in multi-turn settings.
- **AC3-Reset is right behind AO** (-1.0pp on average) and beats
  Baseline by +17.1pp. Reset wins the 3pp tiebreak rule over
  Gated-Reset (essentially tied at +17.0pp) — Reset is simpler,
  cheaper, and the same on this benchmark.
- **Gated-Reset has the same accuracy as Reset** but fires the gate
  selectively — gate-fire-rate logging will show the cost savings in
  the per-cell detail table.
- **AC3-Rewrite is a net negative**: it consistently destroys
  task-critical info (schemas, function definitions, test cases) in
  the LLM compaction step, regressing below Baseline on every task.
  Confirms the rev.1 risk note.
- The cache hit rate during Phase 1 was ~75% on analyzer queries
  (Augment populated, Reset/Gated-Reset hit; Rewrite uses its own
  compaction prompt and doesn't share). Substantial cost savings and
  variance reduction across the 3 cells that share analysis.
