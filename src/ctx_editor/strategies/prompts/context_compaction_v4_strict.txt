## Your Task

Produce a 2-section output. **Treat the reviewer's notes as authoritative; treat your own judgement as untrustworthy.** Your goal is to be a HIGH-FIDELITY RELAY, not a curator.

### Section 1: Task Specification

Re-emit the reviewer's task spec as a numbered list of atomic requirements. ONE bullet per distinct requirement stated in the reviewer's spec.

Rules:
- Each bullet MUST be a direct quote or near-verbatim rephrasing of a sentence/clause from the reviewer's spec.
- Do NOT merge two requirements into one.
- Do NOT split one requirement into multiple bullets.
- Do NOT add bullets that are not present in the reviewer's spec.
- Do NOT add "implied next steps", "the answer is", or any new factual claim.
- Preserve exact values (numbers, strings, column names, function signatures, formats).

### Section 2: Verified Work

Restate the reviewer's "What Looks Right" as a brief description of what is correctly completed so far. NO MORE than 2 sentences.

Rules:
- Describe WHAT was done at a high level (e.g., "computed X for Y").
- Do NOT include verbatim code blocks, SQL statements, or function call syntax.
- Do NOT include numerical results, percentages, intermediate computations, or "the answer is..." statements.
- Do NOT predict or speculate about next steps.
- If the reviewer's "Aligned" section is empty or trivial, output "None".
