## Faithfulness rules — READ CAREFULLY (these make the evaluation valid)
- [Intent is fixed to the shard] The MEANING you convey must be exactly the shard's content —
  the same thing you would mean at entanglement level 0. Entanglement changes only PHRASING and
  INTERPRETABILITY, never intent. The ground-truth answer must be unchanged.
- [RECOVERABLE-WITH-CONTEXT — the key test] Someone who reads BOTH the assistant's most recent
  reply AND your message must be able to reconstruct the FULL shard content EXACTLY and
  unambiguously. Entanglement means "you need the assistant's turn to understand me", NOT "I am
  vague". Do not drop or blur information. Before answering, self-check: given the assistant's
  reply, does my message pin down the entire shard with no guessing? If not, add just enough
  concrete anchoring (a name, an ordinal, the correct quantity) so that it does — while still
  leaning on the assistant's reply so that WITHOUT it the message would be uninterpretable.
- [Reference the assistant for FORM, not for VALUE] You may point at the assistant's reply for
  phrasing (its list, its steps, its approach, "that", "the second one you gave"). You must NOT
  make your intended meaning depend on a specific NUMBER, RESULT, or CLAIM the assistant produced.
  Never encode intent as an arithmetic/logical operation on an assistant-computed value (do NOT say
  "double the number you got" in a way that would mean something different if the assistant's number
  were different). If you refer to a quantity, you mean the CORRECT (shard) quantity, not whatever
  the assistant happened to output.
- [Correct, don't adopt errors] If the assistant's reply is wrong or diverged, you may phrase your
  turn as a correction that points at its mistake ("no, not like that — ..."), but the content you
  assert is still the shard's true content. Do not agree to a wrong claim as if it were true.
- [Degrade gracefully] If the assistant's last reply offers NOTHING usable to attach to at the
  requested level (e.g. it asked a generic question, or was too terse to contain a referent), fall
  back to the nearest lower-severity form rather than fabricating a dependence or going vague.
  Prefer referencing your OWN earlier turns over inventing an assistant referent that isn't there.
  It is better to be a little less entangled than to lose shard information.
- [Full information] Your message (once the reference is resolved against the assistant's reply)
  must carry ALL the shard's information. No dropping content.

## Style rules
- [One shard] Reveal at most ONE shard, the most basic/relevant not-yet-revealed one.
- [No questions] Declarative sentences, not questions.
- [Brevity] Succinct, conversational, a little sloppy — like a real person typing fast.
- [No repeats] Never reveal a shard whose shard_id is already in the revealed list.
