## 0. Answers to the questions asked

**Is a third turn normal?** Yes. NeurIPS runs an author–reviewer *discussion* period, not a single-shot rebuttal; review → author response → reviewer reply → author response is ordinary and expected. Continuing is not a faux pas. What *is* counterproductive: long re-litigation, repeating arguments they have already rejected, and asking for a score change. Norm is one more post, short, addressing only the specific unresolved items, ending with genuine thanks and no ask.

**Should we bother, given they said they'll retain borderline-reject?** Yes, for two reasons.

1. **Their #1 remaining blocker is a thing we can now discharge with data, not argument.** They wrote that *"the promised matched-compute condensation baseline and high-pollution self-reflection experiment are not yet reported."* Half of that is now reported. A reviewer who says "X is missing" and then receives X has a stated reason to move; a reviewer who receives more argument does not.
2. **The AC reads the thread.** Even if Vg97 does not move, the visible record of "asked for a baseline → baseline delivered within the window, including the parts that went against the authors" is what the AC weighs. This post has two audiences and the second one matters more.

**Do we have genuinely new material, or just rhetoric?** New material. Specifically: the matched-compute condensation baseline (their explicit ask), MT-OSC, the item-level/clustered statistics that answer their unit-of-analysis question, and the tau2 re-measurement that answers their Kimi question. The only *rhetorical* move recommended is conceding their framing on concern 2 — and that is a concession, not a defence.

**"Just thank them"?** Insufficient here. A pure thank-you when they have named two concrete blockers, one of which we have since closed, reads as having nothing to offer.

## 4. Decision required before drafting: the two named gaps

Vg97 named **U-Fold** and **reflection-at-high-pollution**. Both are absent. Options:

- **(a) Run reflection on database/code now.** Cheap — same shape as the summarisation arms already built, hours not days, and it directly closes half of a stated blocker. **Recommended.** It also retires the weakest item in our record (a control that was run on near-ceiling math and proved nothing).
- **(b) Run U-Fold on LiC database/code.** They named it twice. But they asked for it *"especially in the tau2 setting"*, and tau2 is being withdrawn — so we can deliver the method but not the venue. Honest framing: run it where we can measure, state plainly that the tau2 comparison is unavailable because we are retracting that benchmark.
- **(c) Neither; scope-argue.** Not recommended. They have already rejected the scoping argument once, in writing: *"this distinction alone is not sufficient to rule out an empirical comparison."* Repeating it is the exact re-litigation that annoys reviewers.

My recommendation: **(a) now, and (b) if there is time.** If neither is possible before the window closes, say explicitly that they are not done and commit to them for camera-ready — a named, dated gap is survivable; an unacknowledged one is not.

## 5. Final AC note — structure

Your advisor's preferred shape, adapted. ~400–600 words, five short blocks:

1. **Problem and why it matters** (2–3 sentences). Multi-turn degradation is large and well documented; the prior fix — omitting assistant turns — works only where the task is recoverable from user messages alone and collapses to 0% where it is not.
2. **Contribution** (3–4 sentences). Treat context as heterogeneous rather than uniformly harmful. State it at the strength the evidence now supports: **a modular family of context-editing operators sharing one analyzer, with at least one operator improving in every evaluated regime** — not "one configuration everywhere". Adopting the more careful claim voluntarily is worth more than defending the stronger one.
3. **What the rebuttal period added** — the section the AC actually wants. Compact list, each with a number:
   - Matched-compute condensation baseline + MT-OSC reimplementation (the AC's own "limited baselines" concern, and Vg97's #1)
   - A direct pollution-removal evaluation independent of task accuracy: constructed-injection (Tier A) and **causal counterfactual span ablation with no detector in the label path** (Tier B)
   - Analyzer-model sensitivity across 5 models / 4 families
   - Item-level and clustered statistics; judge-agreement and position-bias checks; memory contamination measured at zero
   - LiC scaled to n≈113–150/cell with paired significance
4. **What we corrected ourselves** — do not hide this; it is the strongest available signal of rigour, and the AC will see the corrections in the threads regardless. Four lines: the tau2 comparison **withdrawn** after re-measurement showed two of three published baselines did not replicate; an **ERGO denominator error that had deflated a competitor**, found and fixed; the "preserve what's correct" mechanism claim **retracted for both operators** after causal measurement; and several single-run margins withdrawn after replication.
5. **What remains open** (2–3 sentences). U-Fold and reflection-at-high-pollution if still unrun; the unexplained gpt-5.4 tau2 collapse; deploy-time operator selection. Committed for camera-ready.

**Tone.** No score request. No "we believe the paper is now above threshold." The case is made by the list, not by the plea.
